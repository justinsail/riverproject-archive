# The River Project — Archive

A preserved static archive of [theriverproject.org](https://www.theriverproject.org), maintained to honor the legacy of **Melanie Winter** and the work of The River Project on the Los Angeles River watershed.

## Why this exists

The River Project 501(c)(3) was dissolved in 2026. This archive ensures that
Melanie's writing, the organization's research (Taylor Yard, Tahunga Wash,
Water LA, paddle-the-LA-river, biodiversity, climate, and watershed studies),
and its public-facing materials remain freely accessible at no recurring cost.

## How it's hosted

- Static HTML/CSS/images mirrored from the live Squarespace site
- Deployed on **Cloudflare Pages** (free tier, no expiration)
- Donation functionality, contact forms, and newsletter signups have been
  removed (the 501(c)(3) no longer exists; donations are not accepted)
- Typography migrated from Adobe Typekit to Google Fonts (Mulish aliased as
  motiva-sans) so the site has no third-party SaaS dependencies that could
  rot
